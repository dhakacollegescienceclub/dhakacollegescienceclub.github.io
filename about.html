About Dhaka College Science Club

Legacy Since 1996 | #ForClubAndCountry

Our History

Dhaka College Science Club (DCSC) was established in 1996 at the historic Dhaka College campus (Estd. 1841). For nearly three decades, DCSC has been a beacon of scientific curiosity, nurturing thousands of students into renowned researchers, engineers, doctors, and olympiad champions.

Our Mission & Motto

Motto: #ForClubAndCountry. Our mission is "Spreading the knowledge of science to the utmost", promoting analytical thinking, scientific ethics, and nationwide competitiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is membership free?

Yes! DCSC membership is completely free. Any student of Dhaka College can join by filling out the form on the Join Club page.

How do I get a certificate?

Go to the Join Club page, scroll down to the Certificate section, fill in your details and click "Download Certificate".

Who can participate in events?

Most events are open to Dhaka College Intermediate students. Some national competitions are open to all. Check each event for eligibility details.
